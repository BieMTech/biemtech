Home Screen

Your personal dashboard — see your rank, track daily progress, and launch any of the 6 game modes from one central screen.

👤 Profile Header Card

The colourful gradient card at the top is your player identity. It shows:

🏅

Rank & Level

Your current rank title (e.g. "Math Rookie") and numeric level displayed as a badge.

⚡

XP Progress Bar

Shows how much XP you've earned toward the next level, plus a percentage fill bar.

🧒

Player Name & Age

Your display name and the current age group you are playing in.

🖼️

Avatar

Tap the avatar to go to your Profile page. Unlock new avatars in the Shop.

📊 Stats Row

Four animated stat cards sit below the header. Each card spins its icon every few seconds to catch your eye:

⭐

Total Score

Cumulative points earned across all sessions ever played.

🔥

Daily Streak

How many consecutive days you've played. Miss a day and it resets!

🏆

Best Score

Your highest single-session score — your personal record to beat.

💎

Diamonds

Currency earned by playing. Spend in the Shop on avatars and backgrounds.

📅 Daily Progress Bar

The horizontal progress bar shows how many questions you've solved today out of your daily free allowance. When the bar is full, you've completed your daily set.

  • The bar turns green with a ✅ icon when today's free questions are finished.
  • Bonus questions beyond the free limit are tracked separately and shown as an extra badge.
  • The limit resets every day at midnight.
💡 Parents can adjust the daily question limit from the Parent Dashboard.

🎮 The 6 Game Modes

Below the progress bar, six colourful buttons launch each game mode. Tap any to start immediately:

🌟

Daily Challenge

A fresh set of questions every day. Shows ✅ when already completed for today.

🧩

Practice Mode

Choose your operation and drill at your own pace with no timer.

🚀

Speed Mode

Race the clock. Answer as many as you can before time runs out.

🎲

Mixed Challenge

Random mix of all unlocked operations. No setup required.

⚔️

Battle Mode

Two players compete on the same device. Who solves more?

❌

Mistakes Practice

Retry every question you previously got wrong. Badge shows count.

🔗 Top-Right Navigation Icons

Four icon buttons in the top-right corner give quick access to key sections:

  • 👤 Profile — view rank, level, XP, and lifetime stats
  • 🛒 Shop — buy avatars and backgrounds with your coins
  • 📊 Statistics — detailed accuracy, streaks, and activity calendar
  • ⚙️ Settings — sound, language, theme, speed timer, and more

At the bottom of the Home screen you'll also find:

  • 📈 Statistics shortcut button
  • Change Age Group — switch your age at any time
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Mode — PIN-protected parental controls

🎁 Welcome & Daily Login Bonuses

Two special modals can appear when you open the app:

🎉

Welcome Bonus

Shown only the very first time you launch the app. You receive starter coins to spend in the Shop.

📅

Daily Login Bonus

Every day you open the app, a modal shows your login streak and the coins you earned for that day.
