Results Screen

After every session this screen reveals your star rating, score breakdown, coins earned — and a chance to triple them with a rewarded video.

⭐ Star rating system

Your accuracy percentage determines how many stars you earn for that session:

🏆 100% Perfect score — a golden trophy crown replaces the stars. Triggers 150-particle confetti burst!
⭐⭐⭐ 90 – 99% Excellent! Three stars and the win sound plays.
⭐⭐ 60 – 89% Good job. Two stars and the win sound plays.
⭐ < 60% Keep trying! One star — visit Mistakes Practice to improve.

📊 Stats shown on screen

🎯

Score / Total

e.g. "24 / 30" — questions answered correctly out of all questions presented.

📈

Accuracy %

Large percentage shown below the stars — your primary performance indicator.

⏱️

Time Taken

Total session duration in seconds. Speed Mode also shows your run time separately.

✅

Correct / Wrong

Split of right and wrong answers for quick reference.

Below the session stats, three all-time personal records from your Profile are displayed for comparison: Total Score, Best Score, and Current Streak.

💎 Coins earned

A banner at the bottom of the hero card shows how many coins this session earned. Coins are determined by the number of correct answers and any streak bonuses you collected during play.

3× Triple Coins — Watch an Ad

A special 3× button appears if a rewarded video ad is available. Tap it to watch a short ad and receive 3 times the coins you just earned from that session.

▶️

Claim (available)

Tap to watch the ad. Coins are tripled immediately after the video ends.

✅

Used

Already claimed for this session. The button shows a checkmark and is disabled.

🚫

Unavailable

No ad is ready at this moment. The button is greyed out.

⏳

Loading

The ad is being prepared. Wait a moment and try again.

💡 The Triple Coins offer is available once per session. Watching the ad is entirely optional and does not affect your score or stars.

🔁 What to do next

  • Play Again — immediately restarts the same mode you just finished.
  • Home — returns to the Home screen to choose a different mode.
  • Any wrong answers from this session have already been automatically added to your Mistakes Practice queue.
