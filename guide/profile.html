Profile

Your personal identity card — see your rank, level, XP progress, lifetime records, and customise your name and avatar.

🖼️ Avatar & Rank Card

At the top of the screen, a large circular avatar is displayed inside a gradient border coloured by your current rank. Below the avatar you'll see:

  • Rank badge — ✦ Rank Name (e.g. "Math Rookie", "Number Ninja")
  • Level — your current numeric level (Level 1, Level 2, …)

The avatar displays the emoji or graphic you have equipped from the Shop. Tap the avatar or the Shop shortcut button to open the Shop and change it.

✏️ Player Name

Your display name is fully editable:

1

Tap the name input field

The keyboard appears. The current name is pre-filled and ready to edit.

2

Type your new name

Maximum 20 characters. Any combination of letters, numbers, and symbols is allowed.

3

Tap "Save Name"

The button shows a brief saving state, then confirms the change. Your new name immediately appears on the Home screen header card.

⚡ XP & Level Progress

The Experience card shows a detailed breakdown of your progression:

  • Total XP — all experience points earned across every session ever played.
  • Progress bar — animated fill showing how far you are toward the next level.
  • "X / Y XP · Z%" — exact numbers so you always know how close the next level is.
⚡ You earn XP for every correct answer in every mode. Higher-difficulty questions award more XP per correct answer.

📋 Lifetime Stats Grid

Six stat tiles show your all-time personal records:

⭐

Total Score

Sum of all points from every session played.

🏆

Best Score

Highest score achieved in a single session.

⚡

Best Speed Score

Your personal record from Speed Mode sessions.

🔥

Daily Streak

How many consecutive days you've played.

💎

Coins

Current coin balance available to spend in the Shop.

🎂

Age Group

Your currently selected age group.

🛍️ Customise in Shop

A "Customize in Shop" button at the bottom of the Profile screen is a direct shortcut to the Shop where you can unlock new avatars and backgrounds using your coins.
