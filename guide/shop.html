Shop

Use the coins you earn from playing to unlock custom avatars and backgrounds. Personalise your profile and make it your own.

💎 Your Coin Balance

Your current coin total is shown as a 💎 pill in the top-right corner of the Shop screen. Coins are earned automatically while you play — every correct answer, streak bonus, daily login, and rewarded ad contributes to your balance.

▶️ Watch an Ad → Earn Coins

A banner at the top of the shop offers a rewarded video ad. Tap it to watch a short video and instantly receive bonus coins:

▶️

Ad Available

The banner shows "+ X coins" and a play icon. Tap to watch and earn.

🚫

Ad Limit Reached

The banner is greyed out. Ad rewards are capped per day — come back tomorrow.

🗂️ Item Grid

All available items are displayed in a 2-column grid. Each card shows the item emoji/image, its name, a short description, and a status button. The button changes based on the item's current state:

🎁

Free

Price = 0. A green "Free" button with a gift icon. Tap to claim and equip immediately.

💎

Buy (coins available)

You have enough coins. A purple "Buy" button shows the price. Tap → confirmation dialog → purchase.

🔒

Buy (not enough coins)

Insufficient funds. The button is greyed out. Earn more coins by playing sessions or watching ads.

👕

Equip

You own this item but it's not active. Green "Equip" button — tap to set it as your current avatar or background.

✅ Currently equipped items show a green "Equipped" button with a checkmark. This button is disabled — you can't re-equip what's already active.

🛍️ Purchasing an item

1

Tap "Buy"

A confirmation dialog appears: "Are you sure you want to buy [Item] for X coins? You currently have Y coins."

2

Confirm

Tap Confirm. Coins are deducted and the item is added to your owned items collection.

3

Equip

The item button changes to "Equip". Tap it to activate the new avatar or background.
