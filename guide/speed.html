Speed Mode

The clock is ticking! Answer as many questions as you can before time runs out — and the faster you answer, the bigger your bonus.

⏱️ How Speed Mode works

Speed Mode starts a countdown timer the moment the first question appears. Every correct answer earns points plus a speed bonus based on how quickly you answered. The session ends automatically when the timer reaches zero, then the app shows your results.

There is no fixed question count — you answer as many as you physically can before the time expires.

⚙️ Timer duration

The default duration is 30 seconds, but you can change it in Settings → Speed Mode Duration:

⚡

20 seconds

Extremely fast. Only for confident, quick thinkers. Every second counts.

🚀

30 seconds

The default. A balanced challenge for most ages and skill levels.

⏳

40 seconds

A little more breathing room. Good for practising speed without panic.

🕐

60 seconds

Extended run. Aim for a high question count and build up your Best Speed Score.

💨 Speed Bonus

Each correct answer earns base points plus a speed bonus. The faster you tap the correct answer after the question appears, the higher the bonus. An answer in under 2 seconds earns the maximum bonus; slow answers still earn base points but no bonus.

🏆 Your Best Speed Score is tracked separately in Statistics — try to beat your personal record every time!

🎮 On-screen HUD

  • The progress bar doubles as a timer bar — it depletes left-to-right as seconds pass.
  • A seconds counter in the top-centre counts down visually.
  • Score updates live in the top-right with each correct answer.
  • Streak and bonus-diamonds badges appear when you hit milestones mid-run.

🏁 After time expires

When the countdown hits zero, the session ends and the Results Screen shows your total score, accuracy, how many questions you answered, and the total time of the run. Your best speed score is automatically updated if this run beats it.
