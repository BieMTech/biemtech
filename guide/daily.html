Daily Challenge

A fresh batch of questions every single day. Complete it to keep your streak alive and earn daily coins.

📋 How it works

The Daily Challenge is a set of math questions generated fresh each day based on your age group. There is no timer — you answer at your own pace. Once you complete it, the 🌟 button on the Home screen shows a ✅ checkmark until midnight when the next day's challenge unlocks.

🔄 The Daily Challenge resets at midnight every day. You can only complete it once per day.

🎮 During the session

The quiz screen shows the following while you play:

📍

Progress Indicator

A counter at the top shows your position (e.g. "Question 7 / 20") and an animated progress bar fills as you advance.

⭐

Live Score

Your score updates in real time in the top-right corner every time you answer correctly.

🔥

Streak Badge

A fire badge lights up when you answer several questions in a row correctly. Streaks earn bonus coins.

💎

Bonus Coins Badge

A coin badge animates when you earn bonus coins from a streak milestone.

📝 Question & Answer types

The question format matches your age group:

  • Ages 3–4: Picture / icon matching — tap the image that answers the equation.
  • Age 5: Visual arithmetic — small emoji icons grouped to illustrate addition.
  • Ages 6–8: Multiple choice — four labelled buttons (A B C D) with numeric options.
  • Ages 9–10: Typed input — a keyboard appears and you type your numeric answer directly.
💡 For typed input, if your answer is wrong, a hint briefly shows the correct value before the next question loads.

🔥 Daily Streak

Every day you play the Daily Challenge (or any mode), your streak counter increases by 1. Miss a day and it resets to zero. Your streak is displayed on the Home screen stats row and in the Statistics section.

  • Streak counter is shown on the Home screen as 🔥 with a number.
  • Streak milestones can be viewed in the Statistics screen.

🏁 After you finish

When the last question is answered, the app navigates automatically to the Results Screen where you'll see your star rating, score, accuracy, and coins earned.
