Practice Mode

Choose exactly which operation you want to practise. No timer, no pressure — just focused, purposeful math training.

🎯 Setup screen

Before the quiz starts (for ages 6 and above), a setup screen appears with a row of operation pills. Tap one or more to select the type of math you want, then press Start.

Children under age 6 skip this screen — the app starts the quiz automatically using operations appropriate for their age.

🔢 All ➕ Add ➖ Subtract ✖️ Multiply ➗ Divide 🔢 Triple 🔣 Compound

🔓 Which operations unlock at which age?

Operation Unlocks at age Example
➕ Addition 3+ 4 + 3 = ?
➖ Subtraction 6+ 9 − 4 = ?
✖️ Multiplication 7+ 6 × 3 = ?
➗ Division 8+ 12 ÷ 4 = ?
🔢 Triple ops 7–9 2 + 3 + 4 = ?
🔣 Compound ops 10+ 4 × 3 − 5 = ?
🔒 Operation pills for unlocked operations are greyed out and unselectable. Change your age group to unlock them.

🏁 During & after the session

The quiz runs like any other mode — progress bar at the top, live score, streak badge. When all questions are answered the Results Screen appears automatically.

  • Selecting All includes every operation available for your age.
  • Only one operation can be selected at a time.
  • Your coin and XP earnings from Practice sessions count toward your totals.
