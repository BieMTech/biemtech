Theme Selection

The very first screen you see. Pick a visual style that matches your personality — every colour, button, and background adapts to your choice.

✨ What is this screen?

Theme Selection appears the very first time you open Math Kids. It lets you dress the entire app in one of four distinct visual identities. The background, colours, card styles, and glow effects all change based on the theme you choose.

You can change your theme at any time later via Settings → Change Theme, so there is no pressure to pick the "perfect" one right away.

🎨 The 4 Themes

🌿

Forest Explorers

Deep greens and earthy tones. Calm, nature-inspired atmosphere.

✏️

Pencil & Paper

Bright pencil-and-paper style. Friendly, classroom look.

🍬

Kawaii Sweets

Soft pinks and candy pastels. Cute, sweet, and playful.

🚀

Cosmic Adventure

Dark space background with neon glows. Epic and immersive.

🖱️ How to use this screen

1

Tap a theme card

The whole background preview changes instantly — try all four before deciding.

2

Check the selected badge

A ✓ checkmark appears in the top-right corner of the active theme card.

3

Tap Confirm

Press the Confirm button at the bottom to lock in your theme and move to Age Selection.

⚙️ Changing your theme later

You can switch themes at any time after setup:

  • Open the Home screen and tap the gear icon ⚙️ in the top-right corner.
  • Scroll to the Theme / Appearance section.
  • Tap Change Theme to open this screen again.
💡 Changing theme is purely visual — it does not affect your progress, scores, or any game data.
