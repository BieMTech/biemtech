Statistics

Your complete math progress at a glance — accuracy ring, 8 key stats, weekly chart, and a month-by-month activity heatmap.

🔘 Age Filter Pills

A row of horizontally-scrollable pills sits at the top of the screen. Tap a pill to filter all statistics to a specific age group, or tap All to see your combined lifetime data across every age you've played.

📊 All 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 10

🎯 Accuracy Ring

A large circular ring in the centre of the screen shows your overall accuracy percentage. The ring colour signals performance level:

●

Green — ≥ 80%

Strong performer. You're getting most questions right.

●

Orange — 50–79%

Room to improve. Try Mistakes Practice for the weak spots.

●

Red — < 50%

Keep practising. Focus on one operation at a time.

Below the ring, three smaller values show: Correct (green), Wrong (red), and Total (blue) question counts.

🗂️ The 8 Stat Tiles

🔥

Daily Streak

Consecutive days you have played at least one session.

🏆

Best Score

Your highest score across all single sessions ever played.

📅

Login Streak

Consecutive days you have opened the app, regardless of playing.

📆

Total Login Days

Lifetime count of unique days you have opened Math Kids.

💎

Coins

Your current coin balance available to spend in the Shop.

✨

Level

Your current player level based on total XP accumulated.

⚡

Total XP

Total experience points earned across all modes and sessions.

⚡

Best Speed Score

Your personal record from Speed Mode runs.

📈 Level Progress Section

Below the stat tiles, a dedicated progress card shows:

  • Your rank emoji and rank name (e.g. 🧮 "Math Rookie")
  • Level number and current XP
  • An animated progress bar filling toward the next level
  • Text: "X / Y XP · Z%" showing exact progress numbers

📅 Weekly Bar Chart

Seven coloured bars show how many questions you solved on each of the past 7 days (Mon–Sun). Each bar is colour-coded and labelled with the day abbreviation below it. Hover or tap a bar to see the exact count for that day.

🗓️ Activity Heatmap Calendar

A full-month calendar grid shows your activity intensity using five shades:

⬜

0 questions

Empty day — no activity recorded.

🟩

1 – 4 questions

Light green — a little activity.

🟩

5 – 9 questions

Medium green — a solid session.

🟩

10 – 19 questions

Dark green — very active day.

Use the ← → arrows to navigate between months. Today's date is outlined in white for easy reference. A legend at the bottom maps the colour scale (0 → 20+).
