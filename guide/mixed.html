Mixed Challenge

Every operation you've unlocked, shuffled together at random. No setup required — just tap and go.

🎲 What makes it "Mixed"?

The Mixed Challenge automatically combines all the math operations your current age group has unlocked and serves them in a random order. You might get addition, then multiplication, then subtraction — you can never predict what's next.

There is no setup screen. Tap the 🎲 Mixed Challenge button on the Home screen and the quiz starts instantly.

✅ Why play Mixed?

🧠

Keeps you sharp

Switching between operation types forces your brain to stay flexible rather than settling into one pattern.

⚡

Great for daily warm-up

When you've already completed the Daily Challenge, Mixed is the perfect way to keep earning XP and coins.

🏆

Tests true mastery

Being strong at mixed problems means you truly understand all your operations, not just a favourite one.

🎯

Surfaces weak spots

Mistakes from Mixed sessions are saved to your Mistakes Practice queue so you can revisit them.

🎮 During the session

The quiz screen shows your progress bar, live score, streak badge, and bonus-coins counter — identical to the other modes. Answer each question and the app automatically advances to the next random one.

  • Operations used depend entirely on your age group — see the Age Selection guide for the full table.
  • Earning a streak in Mixed is especially rewarding because you're switching operations every question.

🏁 After the session

The Results Screen shows your star rating, score, accuracy percentage, and coins earned. Any wrong answers are added to your Mistakes Practice queue.
