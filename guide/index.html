Getting Started

Up and running in 4 steps

1 📥

Download

Get Math Kids from Google Play — it's free.

2 🎨

Choose a Theme

Pick Forest, Colorful, Kawaii, or Cosmic on first launch.

3 🎂

Select Your Age

Choose age 3–10. The app adjusts difficulty automatically.

4 🚀

Start Playing

Tap any game mode on the Home screen and solve math problems!
