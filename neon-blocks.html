Features

Three ways to master the same board

🧩
8×8 Block Strategy
Drag each piece into open space, complete rows or columns, and keep enough room for the next tray.
🎮
Classic, Gravity and Rush
Play the calm original rule set, let cleared blocks collapse into cascades, or race a 90-second clock.
⚡
Chain-Reaction Cascades
Gravity can complete new lines after a clear, creating multi-wave chains and larger combo scores.
✨
Responsive Neon Feedback
Smooth drag controls, impact ripples, line beams, particles, haptics, and generated audio make every move readable.
📊
Saved Runs and Statistics
Each mode keeps its own best score and paused run, while lifetime statistics track your progress.
🔒
Private and Offline
The current build works offline with no account, real ads, external analytics, purchases, or personal-data collection.
