Features

A familiar classic, brought to life in 3D

🏝️
A Growing Adventure
Clear levels on an expanding floating island with new obstacle layouts and a star rating for every win.
🐍
Classic Mode
Chase a new high score on a fixed board as the snake grows longer and the pace gets faster.
⚡
Swipe, Steer, Boost
Smooth swipe controls on mobile and keyboard controls on desktop make every quick turn feel natural.
🍎
Combos and Surprises
Build score combos, catch the rare golden bird, collect extra hearts, and revive when a run gets tough.
📊
Skins and Statistics
Choose from three snake styles and track your best score, longest snake, biggest combo, and total play time.
🌍
Private and Offline
Play offline in 12 languages. No account, advertising, analytics, or personal-data collection.
