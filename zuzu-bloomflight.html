Zuzu: Bloomflight

Float. Bloom. Explore.

Guide Zuzu through six changing gardens. Tap to fly, hold to hover, bloom flowers, manage nectar, collect power-ups, and chase a new high score.

Features

Every garden changes your rhythm

🐝
One-Touch Flight
Tap to flap and hold to hover. Simple one-handed controls turn timing and limited nectar into a focused arcade challenge.
🌼
Hover With Purpose
Hover near buds to open flowers and widen the path, avoid draining nectar beside angry hives, and recharge inside the Honey Core.
🌿
Six Living Gardens
Cross blossom gates from sunflower fields and lavender rows to rose thorns, a greenhouse, Wasp Hollow, and the golden Honey Core.
🛡️
Three Power-Ups
A shield absorbs one collision, mega nectar grants five seconds of unlimited hovering, and slow time gives you room to react.
🏆
Perfect Passes and Stats
Thread the center of each gap for perfect bonuses, build combos, chase your best score, and track games, flowers bloomed, and gardens seen.
🔒
Private and Offline
The current build plays offline in 12 languages, with no account, ads, analytics, purchases, social features, or personal-data collection.
