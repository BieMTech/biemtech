REX: Dragon Flight

Guide Rex through ten changing worlds with a tap to fly and a hold to breathe fire. Melt ice, burn wood, trigger explosive chains, collect power-ups, and adapt when fire meets metal.

Features

Every world changes how you fly

🐉
One-Touch Flight
Tap to flap and hold to breathe fire. Simple one-handed controls turn timing and fuel into a fast arcade challenge.
🔥
Fire With Consequences
Burn wood quickly, melt ice to widen gaps, ignite explosive chains, and save your fuel when metal blocks the flame.
🌋
Ten Distinct Worlds
Cross portals from volcanoes and crystal deserts to iron keeps, storm peaks, and the Void—each with its own look, music, and rules.
🛡️
Three Power-Ups
A shield absorbs one collision, mega flame grants five seconds of unlimited fire, and slow time gives you room to react.
🏆
Perfect Runs and Stats
Thread the center of each gap for perfect bonuses, build combos, chase your best score, and track games, burned obstacles, and discovered worlds.
🔒
Offline Core, Optional Leaderboard
Core play works offline in 12 languages. Google Play Games sign-in is optional and is used only for the online leaderboard; there are no ads, analytics, or purchases.
