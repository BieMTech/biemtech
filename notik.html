Features

Make room for your everyday ideas

✎

Write it your way

Create rich-text notes and checklists. Organize them with categories, tags, colors, pins, and archive.

◈

Go beyond text

Attach chosen photos, draw on a canvas, import audio, or record a voice note when you want to capture more.

◷

Remember what matters

Set one-time or repeating reminders and use the calendar to see what is coming up.

↗

Keep your own copies

Export notes as PDF or text, share through apps you choose, and export or restore a local JSON backup.
