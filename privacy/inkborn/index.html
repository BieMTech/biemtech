INKBORN

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Android package: com.biemtech.inkborn · In development

About this policy

Inkborn is an Android game by BieMTech. This policy describes the current development version. For privacy questions, contact info@biemtech.com.

Data stored on your device

The game stores progress, crystals, unlocked items, daily rewards and settings in local app storage. If you choose Save Painting, it also saves an image of the match in app storage. The game does not require an account or send these files to BieMTech. Device backup settings may affect copies of app data.

Ads and purchases

The current development version does not have an advertising SDK or real-money purchases. Items in the game shop are obtained using crystals earned in the game. A future advertising feature is currently disabled. If data practices change, this policy will be updated before release.

Retention and deletion

Local saves and paintings remain in app storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to device backups. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently erase your progress and saved paintings. Android provides app storage protections.

Contact and changes

If you email info@biemtech.com, we use the details you provide to respond. Your app store and operating system may process information under their own privacy policies. We will update this policy and its effective date if the game's data practices change.
