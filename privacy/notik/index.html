Notik

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Developer: BieMTech · Android package: com.biemtech.notepad

Notik is in development. Your notes and media are stored on your device. The current app has no account system, advertising, analytics, or BieMTech cloud sync. Sharing, exported files, and device backups are described below.

How to delete your data

1. About this policy

BieMTech develops Notik, a note-taking app with checklists, drawings, voice notes, and reminders. This policy covers the current Notik Android app and its browser-based version. BieMTech does not receive your note content through normal use of the app. We will update this policy if its data practices change.

2. Data stored on your device

Notik uses local app or browser storage to keep the information needed for its features:

  • Note text, titles, checklist items, categories, tags, colors, and creation and edit dates.
  • Photos, audio files, voice recordings, and drawings that you choose to add.
  • Reminder times, repeat settings, and the note identifier used to open a reminder.
  • Your display and language preferences, plus a salt and hashed PIN or recovery answer if you enable a lock. The PIN itself is not stored as plain text.

These records support your notebook on the device. The current app does not automatically send them to BieMTech or an analytics service.

3. Permissions and optional features

  • Microphone: used when you start recording a voice note. You can stop recording and revoke microphone permission in your device or browser settings.
  • Photos, camera, and files: system or browser pickers let you choose a photo, take a picture where supported, or import an audio or backup file. Selected content is copied into your local notebook.
  • Notifications and alarms: used for local reminders. Unlocked notes can show a title and a short preview in notifications, including on your lock screen depending on your device settings. Locked notes hide the note title and preview.
  • Storage, vibration, and startup handling: used where supported to save exported files, give haptic feedback, and restore scheduled reminders after a device restart.

You can manage permissions in system settings. Denying an optional permission can disable the related feature. Notik does not request access to your contacts or location.

4. Sharing and exported backups

When you choose Share, the selected text or file is passed to the app or recipient you select, or copied to your clipboard when sharing is unavailable. That recipient or service handles the shared information under its own policies.

PDF, text, and JSON exports are saved to a location you choose or your device's documents/downloads area. JSON backups include the notes, categories, and attached media in your database, including locked or trashed notes. Exported files are not encrypted by Notik. Keep them somewhere you trust; deleting a note in Notik does not delete an exported or shared copy.

5. Retention and deletion

Notes remain on your device until you remove them. Deleted notes first go to Trash. You can restore them or delete them permanently there. By default, notes in Trash expire after 30 days; Settings lets you choose 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days. Expired notes and their attached media are removed when the app next starts.

To remove the local notebook and preferences, clear Notik's app data in Android settings or clear the site's stored data in your browser. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but exported files, copies shared with other apps, and system backups may remain. Delete those separately through the relevant device, backup, or service settings.

Notik has no user accounts to delete. BieMTech cannot remotely read, recover, or delete notes kept only on your device. For help with data deletion, contact info@biemtech.com.

6. PIN locks and security

The optional PIN lock controls access within the app. It does not encrypt the notes, attached media, or exported backups. Your device's security and backup settings also affect protection. Use a device screen lock and take care when sharing files or lending access to your device.

7. Device backups and transfer

The Android app allows operating-system backup. Depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, and backup settings, the system may back up or transfer app data through Google or your device provider. This is separate from Notik's manual JSON export and is not a BieMTech cloud service. Manage backup, restore, and deletion through your device or backup provider's settings.

8. Services, support, and this website

The current Notik app has no advertising, tracking, automated crash-reporting, or account service. BieMTech does not sell your note content. If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send and use them to handle your request and related follow-up. Avoid sending private notes unless needed. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

This policy and the Notik project page are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening a website or an external link sends ordinary web request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to its host. Our pages also store your selected website language in your browser. Hosting, email, device backup, and sharing providers apply their own privacy policies.

GitHub Privacy Statement

9. Children

Notik is a general-purpose notebook. It does not request a user's age, create child profiles, or intentionally collect children's note content on BieMTech servers. If a parent or guardian is concerned about personal information sent to us through support, contact us so we can review and address the request.

10. Changes and contact

Notik is in development, and features may change before public release. We will revise this policy and its effective date when data handling changes. For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, contact BieMTech:

info@biemtech.com
