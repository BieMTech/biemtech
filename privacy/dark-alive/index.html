Privacy Policy

Developer: BieMTech · App: Dark Alive (Wallpaper Alive) · Android package: com.wallpaperalive.wallpaper_alive · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Dark Alive does not collect or send personal information to BieMTech. Its wallpaper gallery and playback work offline. Favorites and selected wallpaper files are kept on your device. Saving or sharing a wallpaper happens only when you choose those actions.

Information and local storage

The app does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera, or microphone. It stores the IDs of wallpapers you mark as favorites in app preferences. When you apply a live wallpaper, it copies the selected bundled video into the app's private storage so Android can display it. The app does not send this information or your activity to BieMTech servers.

Wallpaper access, saving, and sharing

Dark Alive uses Android's wallpaper functions to show a preview and apply a wallpaper when you request it. If you choose Save, the selected video is copied to your device's Movies/Dark Alive folder, where it may be available to other apps with media access. If you choose Share, the app gives the selected video and its title to the app or service you choose through Android's share interface. That recipient handles the shared content under its own privacy practices. Dark Alive does not automatically share your favorites or other personal information.

Internet, third parties, and backups

The current app has no ads, analytics, tracking, account system, in-app purchases, or app-operated cloud service. Its wallpaper content is bundled with the app. Android and Google Play may process information for installation, device services, and optional device backups under their own policies. If Android backup is enabled on your device, app preferences and private app files may be backed up or restored by the platform. BieMTech does not receive that backup data.

Retention, deletion, and security

Favorites and selected wallpaper copies remain in app storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to any Android backup and restore settings. Videos you saved to Movies/Dark Alive remain there until you delete them separately. Shared copies are controlled by the recipient you chose. App-private data is protected by Android's app storage controls; the app does not transmit it to BieMTech.

Children and policy changes

Dark Alive does not offer registration, chat, targeted advertising, or social profiles, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its date before the relevant version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact BieMTech at info@biemtech.com.
