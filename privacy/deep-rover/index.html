Deep Rover

Privacy Policy · Ages 13+

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Android package: com.biemtech.deeprover · In development

About this policy

Deep Rover is an Android game by BieMTech, intended for people aged 13 and over. This policy describes the current development version. Contact us at info@biemtech.com with privacy questions.

Data on your device

The game stores progress, currency, upgrades, discovered artifacts and settings in a local save file. It does not require an account. The current game does not send the save to BieMTech. Device backup settings may affect copies of app data.

Motion sensor

If you enable tilt control, the game reads the device accelerometer during play. These readings are used for control and are not saved or sent to BieMTech.

Ads, purchases and analytics

The current development version uses simulated ads and purchases for testing; no real ad network or payment service is connected. The analytics hook has no backend and may print events in development builds. If real services are added, their data practices will be described in an updated policy before release.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to device backups. The in-game reset clears progress but retains settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may permanently remove your progress. Android provides app storage protections.

Children under 13

Deep Rover is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact info@biemtech.com so we can address it.

Contact and changes

If you email info@biemtech.com, we use the information you provide to respond. App stores and operating systems may process information under their own policies. We will update this policy and its effective date if the game's data practices change.
