Kitefall

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Android package: com.biemtech.kitefall
Kitefall does not require an account. Game progress and settings are saved locally on your device; the game does not send them to BieMTech.

Who we are

Kitefall is an Android game by BieMTech. This policy describes the current version of the game. For privacy questions, contact info@biemtech.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your route progress, scores, best distances, completed challenges, Windglass balance, owned and equipped gear, tutorial progress, and sound and motion settings in its local app storage. This lets you resume playing. The game does not ask for your name, email address, or an account.

Collection and sharing

The current game does not include a BieMTech account or online game service. It does not transmit your saved progress to BieMTech or other companies. It does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

The current game does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not offer in-app purchases. Windglass and gear are game items with no cash value.

App stores and device services

If you install Kitefall through an app store, the store and your device's operating system may process information under their own privacy policies. Your device's backup settings may also affect copies of locally stored app data. These services are separate from the game's local save.

Retention, security, and deletion

Your save remains in the app's local storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup settings. The game relies on Android's app storage protections. Clearing storage or uninstalling the game may permanently erase your progress.

Children's privacy

Kitefall does not require an account or ask children for personal information. BieMTech does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game.

Contact and changes

If you email us at info@biemtech.com, we use the details you provide to respond to your inquiry. If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
