Wildwood Drive Privacy Policy

BieMTech · Android · com.biemtech.wildwooddelivery

Last updated 9 November 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the current development version of Wildwood Drive for Android. The game works offline and does not require an account.

What the game stores

The game stores your stars, unlocked vehicles and regions, upgrades, distance records, and settings in app storage so you can resume play. It keeps a local recovery copy and may retain a copy of an older Wildwood Delivery save when upgrading it.

Data sent from the game

BieMTech does not receive your game saves. The current game has no BieMTech account, server-side progress, ads, in-app purchases, or gameplay analytics integration.

Depending on your device and settings, Android may back up or transfer app data through its own services. Android backup and restore

Reset or delete your data

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game to start over while keeping your preferences. To remove the local save and its recovery copies, clear Wildwood Drive’s app storage in Android settings. Manage Android backups separately in your device settings.

Contact and updates

If you email us, BieMTech uses your email address and the details you provide to respond. We will update this page if the game’s data practices change.
