MathKids Privacy Policy

Android package: com.biemtech.funmathkids · Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short: MathKids is a maths game for children aged 5–8. The current app has no account, ads or in-app purchases and does not send gameplay data to BieMTech. It saves age, settings, level and stars on the device. Android backup may copy those settings and progress to a backup service or transfer them to a new device, depending on device settings.

What the app stores

MathKids asks the child to choose an age (5, 6, 7 or 8). It stores the selected age, chosen language, sound and music settings, and level and star totals for each age in the app's private storage. It uses these values to show age-appropriate questions and remember progress. The app does not ask for a name, email address, location or account.

Data sharing and network access

The current app does not request internet access and does not include an advertising, analytics, billing or crash-reporting SDK. BieMTech does not receive gameplay or progress data from the app. There are no third-party ads or behavioural tracking in the current build.

Android backup and device transfer

The app allows Android to back up and transfer its private preferences, including the selected age, settings, level and stars. If backup is enabled on the device, the operating system or the user's backup provider may process a copy under its own settings and privacy terms. BieMTech does not operate that backup service or access its contents.

Permissions and temporary files

The app requests vibration permission to give feedback during play. It generates sound effects and music on the device and keeps temporary audio files in the app's cache. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permission.

Children and parents

MathKids is designed for children aged 5–8. A parent or guardian can change the age and language in settings. To remove locally saved progress and settings, clear MathKids app data in Android Settings or uninstall the app. If Android backup is enabled, a backup copy may remain with the device's backup provider according to that provider's retention rules.

Updates and contact

If app features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. For privacy questions, email info@biemtech.com.
