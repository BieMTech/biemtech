Privacy Policy

LiD Trainer

Android study app · In development

Developer: BieMTech · Android package: com.biemtech.lid · Effective date: 21 October 2026 · Privacy contact: info@biemtech.com
LiD Trainer works offline and does not send your study data to BieMTech. Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If Android backup is enabled, the system may back them up to your Google account or transfer them to a new device.

1. Scope and developer

This policy describes the current development version of LiD Trainer, an independent Android study app by BieMTech for the Leben in Deutschland and Einbürgerungstest. The app is not affiliated with BAMF. We will update this page before release if its data practices change.

2. Data the app uses and stores

The app stores your selected Bundesland, study goal and exam date, translation and display preferences, reminder settings, bookmarks, per-question answers and response times, study activity, and mock-exam results in Android app storage. These records are used to personalize practice, show statistics, and let you resume studying. The app does not ask for a name, email address, account, location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

3. Data sent to us and third parties

The current app does not request internet access and has no BieMTech account, server sync, ads, analytics, or in-app purchases. BieMTech does not receive your study records. If you use read-aloud, Android passes the question text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device; how that engine handles text depends on its provider and settings. The app does not send text to BieMTech.

4. Android backup and device transfer

The app currently allows Android Auto Backup of its progress database and settings. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may copy this data to your Google account for restoration or transfer it directly to another device. This is handled by Android and your backup provider, not by a BieMTech server. You can manage backups in your device or Google account settings.

5. Permissions and reminders

Optional daily reminders use Android notifications. On supported versions, the app asks for notification permission when you enable reminders. It uses boot completion to reschedule them after a restart and vibration for optional haptic feedback. You can disable reminders in the app and notifications in Android settings.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Study data remains in app storage until you reset progress, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. Reset Progress removes study records but keeps your preferences; clearing storage removes both. An Android backup may remain under your backup provider's retention rules, so manage or delete backups through your device or account settings. BieMTech has no server copy to delete.

7. Children and security

The app is a general study aid. It has no registration, chat, public profile, or targeted advertising, and BieMTech does not knowingly collect personal data from children through the app. Study records are kept in Android app storage, which is protected by the device's app sandbox; backup protection is managed by Android and the backup provider.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's features or data practices change. Please review this page when the app is released or updated.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact BieMTech at info@biemtech.com.
