Neon Blocks

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026 · Package: com.biemtech.neonblocks

Privacy at a glance: Neon Blocks does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The game works offline and contains no advertising, analytics, accounts, or social features.

1. About the game

Neon Blocks is an offline block puzzle game published by BieMTech. It offers Gravity, Classic, and Rush game modes.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or device identifiers. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

3. Information stored on your device

The game stores only the information required for local gameplay, including:

  • scores, best scores, achievements, and current game progress;
  • selected language and gameplay settings;
  • sound and haptic feedback preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to BieMTech or any third party.

4. Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Neon Blocks contains no advertisements and uses no advertising, analytics, tracking, or social-media SDKs. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

5. Permissions and network access

The game does not request Internet access. It may request vibration permission solely to provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

6. Children’s privacy

Neon Blocks is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children. The game has no accounts, chat, user-generated content, purchases, or behavioral advertising.

7. Data deletion

Because gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can delete it using the game’s Reset Progress option, Android’s Clear Storage function, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to BieMTech.
