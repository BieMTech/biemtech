Sky Hook Privacy Policy

For players ages 13+

Last updated 9 November 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the current development version of Sky Hook for Android. You can play offline without creating an account.

Minimum age and children’s privacy

Players must be at least 13 years old. BieMTech does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 sent us personal information, email us so we can review and remove it. info@biemtech.com

Data stored on your device

Sky Hook saves your unlocked stages, best heights, stars, coins, gems, gear upgrades, boosters, outfits, and settings in app storage so you can resume play. The game may keep a copy of a save file it cannot read. BieMTech does not receive these files.

Depending on your device and settings, Android may back up or transfer app data through its own services. Android backup and restore

Test ads, purchases, and logs

The current version simulates rewarded ads and purchases on your device. The test screens do not contact an ad network, request payment details, or charge you. Developer test builds can write gameplay events to local logs. Those events have no analytics server.

Deleting your saved data

To delete your local progress and settings, clear Sky Hook’s app storage in Android settings. You can manage Android backups separately in your device settings.

Contact and updates

If you email us, BieMTech uses your email address and the details you provide to respond. We will update this page if the game’s data practices change.
