Pocket Highway

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Android package: com.biemtech.pockethighway
Pocket Highway does not require an account. The game saves your progress on your device and does not send it to BieMTech.

Who we are

Pocket Highway is an Android game by BieMTech. This policy describes the current version of the game. For privacy questions, contact info@biemtech.com.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game stores race results and records, credits, car ownership and appearance, daily challenge and mission progress, and your control and audio settings in local app storage. It does not ask for your name, email address, or an account. BieMTech does not receive this saved game data.

Tilt steering

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your device's motion sensor to steer the car. Sensor readings are used during play and are not stored in your save file or sent to BieMTech.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

The current game does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not offer in-app purchases. Credits and cars are game items and cannot be exchanged for money.

Sharing and platform services

The game does not send your progress or sensor readings to BieMTech or other companies. If you install the game through an app store, that store and your device's operating system may process information under their own privacy policies. Device backup settings may also affect copies of locally stored app data.

Retention, security, and deletion

Your save stays in the app's local storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup settings. The game relies on Android's app storage protections. Clearing storage or uninstalling the game may permanently erase your progress.

Children's privacy

The game does not require an account or ask children for personal information. BieMTech does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game.

Contact and changes

If you email us at info@biemtech.com, we use the details you provide to respond to your inquiry. If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
