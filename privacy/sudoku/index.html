Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Android package: com.biemtech.sudoku
The current development version of Sudoku does not collect or transmit personal information. Your game progress is stored on your device.

About this policy

Sudoku is an offline puzzle game being developed by BieMTech. This policy describes the current Android development version. We will update it before release if the app's data practices change.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings, active puzzles, daily challenge results, journey progress, statistics, and achievements locally so you can continue playing. It does not require an account, name, or email address. This saved information is not sent to BieMTech or third parties.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

The current development version has no advertisements, analytics service, or in-app purchases.

Device features and permissions

The current Android configuration does not request internet access. The game can use vibration for optional haptic feedback and can read the device language and appearance settings to adapt its interface. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect or share personal information from children. Local game progress stays on the device.

Your choices

You can reset statistics in the game settings. To remove all locally stored game data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. These actions may erase your progress.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will revise this page and its effective date before the relevant changes are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@biemtech.com.
