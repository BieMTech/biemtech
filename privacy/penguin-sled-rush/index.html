Penguin Sled: Aurora Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Penguin Sled: Aurora Run (package name com.biemtech.penguinrush) is published by Biemtech (Bahram Ismayilov). This policy explains how the game handles information.

Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, or other personal details. Scores, progress, missions, unlocked items, and settings are stored locally on your device so the game can remember them. The game does not transmit this information to us or to third parties.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

The current version does not show advertisements, use an analytics service, or offer in-app purchases. Fish and other rewards earned while playing are game-only items and cannot be exchanged for money.

Device permissions

The current Android version does not request internet access. It may use your device's vibration feature for haptic feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect or share personal information from children. Local game progress stays on the device.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the game. These actions may erase your progress.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant changes are released. The effective date above will also be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@biemtech.com.
