Penguin Sled: Aurora Run

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Android package: com.biemtech.penguinrush
Penguin Sled: Aurora Run does not ask for an account or collect personal information. Game progress is stored on your device.

About the game

Penguin Sled: Aurora Run is an offline 3D game published by BieMTech. This policy describes how the current Android version handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves scores, progress, missions, unlocked items, and settings locally so you can continue playing. It does not require your name, email address, or an account. The game does not send this saved information to BieMTech or third parties.

Ads, analytics, and purchases

The current version does not show advertisements, use an analytics service, or offer in-app purchases. Fish and other rewards are game-only items with no monetary value.

Device permissions

The current Android version does not request internet access. It may use device vibration for haptic feedback. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect or share personal information from children. Local game progress stays on the device.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. These actions may erase your progress.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy before the relevant changes are released and revise the effective date above.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@biemtech.com.
