Coil

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Working title: Coil · Android packages: com.biemtech.coil (online), com.coil.unity (offline)

Coil is in development. Online play sends a chosen nickname, a game profile identifier, gameplay events, and scores to the game server. The separate offline Android build keeps progress on your device.

Who this policy covers

BieMTech develops Coil, a real-time multiplayer snake game. This policy covers the current web game, the native online Android client, and the separate offline Android build. Features and data practices may change before release; we will update this page when they do.

Information used for online play

You choose a nickname; no account or email address is required. The online clients send your nickname, a locally generated profile identifier, selected appearance, movement inputs, and gameplay events to the game server so it can run the shared arena. Other players can see your nickname and appearance during play. Your nickname and score can appear on live and saved leaderboards.

Data saved on your device

The web game uses browser storage for your profile identifier, nickname, appearance and sound settings, achievements, and daily challenge progress. The native online client saves preferences on the device. The separate offline Android build stores settings, scores, and progress on the device and does not need an online game session. Clearing browser storage or app data can remove local progress and create a new profile identifier.

Leaderboards and retention

For online leaderboards, the server saves a profile identifier, display nickname, best score, and the time of the run. Daily entries are removed after about 60 days; monthly and all-time records do not currently have an automatic expiry. Each period keeps only its top 50 entries. Removing data from your device does not remove a server leaderboard entry; contact us if you want us to review or remove one.

Diagnostics and network information

The web client reports up to five uncaught errors per page load to our server. A report can include the error message and stack, page path, browser user-agent, and the IP address visible to the server. Game and hosting connections also expose technical data such as an IP address to operate and protect the service. Error reports go to server logs and are used to diagnose faults; their retention depends on the server's log rotation settings.

Ads, purchases, and service providers

The current game builds have no in-game advertising or purchases. Online play requires network and hosting providers to transmit and host game traffic and server data. We do not publish your profile identifier on the leaderboard; displayed nicknames and scores are public to other players.

Your choices and requests

You can use a nickname that does not identify you. You can clear local game data through browser or Android settings, or uninstall an app. To ask about access, correction, or removal of server-held data, email us with your nickname and, if available, your profile identifier so we can locate the record. We will handle requests under applicable law.

Children

Coil does not ask for a real name or email address. Players should avoid putting personal information in a public nickname. If a parent or guardian is concerned about a child's leaderboard entry, contact us so we can review it.

Changes and contact

We will revise this policy and its effective date when the game's data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact BieMTech at

info@biemtech.com.
